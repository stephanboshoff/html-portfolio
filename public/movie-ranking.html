Stephan Boshoff's Favorite Films

The top 3 movies of all time according to Stephan

The Accountant

A gripping thriller about a brilliant accountant who uses his skills to navigate the dangerous world of organized crime.

The Accountant 2

A thrilling sequel that delves deeper into the life of the enigmatic accountant, uncovering new secrets and challenges.

Man of Steel

A superhero film that reimagines the origin story of Superman, exploring his journey to becoming a hero and the challenges he faces along the way.
